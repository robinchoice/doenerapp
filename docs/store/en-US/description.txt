Where's the best döner near you? Lieblingsdöner tells you honestly: people from your area rate every shop by sauce, meat, salad and bread, in half points. That makes your city's ranking.

DISCOVER SHOPS
• The map shows every döner shop around you
• The ranking shows the best in your city and which shop just opened
• Vegetarian and vegan too

RATE HONESTLY
• Sauce, meat, salad and bread separately instead of one star rating
• A single 5 doesn't beat thirty 4.5s
• Price, photos and a few words about your döner

CHECK IN WITH FRIENDS
• One tap on site: your friends see where you're eating
• Connect by QR code and react to their ratings
• At the end of the year you get your Döner Wrapped

FAIR
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Servers in Germany, delete your account in the app at any time
• Notifications only about friends and new shops, each one can be switched off

Sign in with an email code, no password. The app is in German.
